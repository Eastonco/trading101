## Better Strategies for Age 26

### Strategy 1: House Hacking (RECOMMENDED)
**Best option for getting started:**
- Buy 2-4 unit property with FHA (3.5% down) or Conventional (5% down)
- Live in one unit, rent the others
- Get owner-occupied interest rates (6-6.5% vs 7.5%)
- After 1 year, move out and rent your unit too

**Example:**
- Buy duplex for $350K
- FHA loan: $17.5K down (3.5%)
- Monthly mortgage: $2,200
- Rent other unit: $1,500
- Your housing cost: $700/month
- After moving out: Both units rented = $3,000/month = $800 positive cash flow
- **Capital deployed: $25K, keeps $155K liquid**

### Strategy 2: Better Markets
Look at growing Midwest/Southeast markets:
- Indianapolis
- Kansas City
- Memphis
- Jacksonville

**Target:** $150-200K properties renting for $1,500-2,000/month
- Actually hit 1% rule
- Less management complexity
- Easier exit strategy
- Diverse economies

### Strategy 3: Scale Over Time
Build portfolio one property at a time:

**4-Year Plan:**
- Age 26: Property #1 (house hack) → $200/month cash flow
- Age 27: Property #2 → $300/month cash flow
- Age 28: Property #3 → $300/month cash flow
- Age 29: Property #4 → $400/month cash flow

**By Age 30:**
- Cash flow: $1,200/month = $14,400/year
- Appreciation (3%/year on $800K): $24,000/year
- Mortgage paydown: ~$15,000/year
- **Total wealth building: $53,000/year**

**By Age 40:**
- Portfolio value: $1.5M+
- Equity: $800K+
- Passive income stream established

**This strategy only works because you're 26 - can't start at 40.**
