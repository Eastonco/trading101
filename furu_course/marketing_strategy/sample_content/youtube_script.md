## ETHICS NOTE

This webinar script demonstrates:

1. **Emotional Manipulation:** Story designed to build connection and relate to desperation
2. **False Scarcity:** "Only 100 spots" and countdown timers are fabricated
3. **Value Inflation:** "$3,700 value" is completely made up
4. **Minimal Education:** 10 minutes of teaching in "90-minute training"
5. **Pressure Tactics:** 15-minute timer, urgency, FOMO
6. **Social Proof Manipulation:** Assistant posts fake "Just joined!" messages
7. **Misleading Claims:** $50K/month presented as normal/achievable
8. **Cherry-Picked Results:** Only show winners, hide 95%+ failure rate
9. **Blame Shifting Built-In:** "System works if you work it" = your fault if it fails

**Conversion Psychology:**
- 80 minutes of emotional buildup
- Time investment creates sunk cost
- Urgency prevents rational evaluation
- Social proof creates FOMO
- Price anchoring ($1,997 → $97) makes it seem like bargain

**Reality:**
- Course contains generic information available free online
- $97 is pure profit (minimal production costs)
- Most students will not succeed (trading has 90% failure rate)
- Real goal is upselling to $997, $7,500, $25,000 tiers

This script is optimized to bypass rational thinking and trigger emotional, impulsive purchases from financially desperate individuals. Every element is designed to manipulate, not educate.
