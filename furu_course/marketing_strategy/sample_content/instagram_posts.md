## ETHICS NOTE

These Instagram posts demonstrate:

1. **Fabricated Lifestyle:** Rented luxury items presented as owned
2. **Faked Proof:** Screenshots easily manipulated
3. **Cherry-Picked Results:** Only show wins, never losses
4. **Fake Testimonials:** Paid actors or completely fabricated
5. **Artificial Scarcity:** "Closing enrollment" happens monthly
6. **Psychological Manipulation:** Every word chosen to exploit vulnerabilities
7. **False Promises:** Imply easy wealth through subtle messaging

**Key Manipulation Tactics:**
- Aspiration creation (show luxury)
- Authority building (appear expert)
- Social proof (fake testimonials)
- Urgency/scarcity (pressure to buy)
- Emotional manipulation (FOMO, dissatisfaction, hope)

**Target:** Financially desperate individuals scrolling social media

**Goal:** Convert followers into paying customers through systematic psychological manipulation

**Reality:** 95%+ of students will lose money and not achieve promised results

These posts are designed to exploit human psychology for profit, targeting vulnerable populations through sophisticated manipulation tactics. The lifestyle, results, and testimonials are largely fabricated, but presented as authentic to build trust and drive sales.
