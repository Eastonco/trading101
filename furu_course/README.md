## Overview

This project documents the complete business model of a fictional "millionaire day trader" guru course that promises to teach people how to get rich through day trading. While marketed as requiring "hard work," it subtly promotes get-rich-quick thinking through psychological manipulation and false promises.

The materials demonstrate how unethical businesses generate substantial profits (often $1M+ annually) while providing little to no actual value, primarily targeting financially desperate individuals.

## Real-World Context

While this specific course is fictional, the tactics documented here are based on actual patterns observed across numerous online "guru" schemes in:
- Day trading and forex education
- Cryptocurrency investment "secrets"
- E-commerce/dropshipping "systems"
- Real estate "strategies"
- Social media marketing "blueprints"

Common elements include:
- Renting luxury items (cars, jets, homes) for photo shoots
- Manipulating screenshots of "earnings"
- Creating artificial scarcity and urgency
- Using testimonials from paid actors or cherry-picked successes
- Offering tiered products that maximize customer spending
- Pivoting between trends (crypto → NFTs → AI → trading)

## Financial Reality

**What victims believe:**
- Small investment ($97-$997) will teach them to make thousands per day
- "Anyone can do this" with the right system
- The instructor genuinely wants to help them succeed

**Actual reality:**
- 90%+ of day traders lose money (industry data)
- Course content is generic information available for free
- Business model depends on high volume sales, not student success
- Instructor profits regardless of student outcomes
- Most students never implement or succeed, but blame themselves

## Ethical Considerations

This business model is considered unethical because it:

1. **Exploits vulnerability:** Targets people in financial distress
2. **Makes false promises:** Claims of easy wealth that don't materialize
3. **Uses psychological manipulation:** Exploits cognitive biases and desperation
4. **Lacks transparency:** Hides true success rates and value
5. **Prioritizes extraction:** Designed to maximize revenue extraction vs. value delivery
6. **Causes harm:** Leaves victims with debt, shame, and damaged trust
