## ETHICS NOTE

This course outline reveals the core deception:

**The Product:** Generic information available free online, repackaged as "proprietary system"

**The Promise:** Life-changing wealth, financial freedom, quit your job

**The Reality:** Basic education that won't lead to trading success for 95%+ of students

**Key Deceptions:**
1. **False Scarcity of Information:** "Secret patterns" are public knowledge
2. **Inflated Value:** "$3,700 value" for content worth $0-50
3. **Missing Components:** Only 10 of 50 promised patterns
4. **No Real System:** Too vague to implement
5. **False Hope:** 90% of day traders lose money (never mentioned)
6. **Blame Shifting:** Vague enough that failure can be blamed on student

**Why Students Fail:**
- Not because they "didn't work hard enough"
- Not because they "didn't follow the system"
- But because:
  - Day trading has 90% failure rate
  - Course provides no edge
  - Content is too generic to be actionable
  - No real support or mentorship
  - False expectations set by marketing

**The Real Product:**
What's being sold isn't education—it's hope. Students are buying the dream of financial freedom, not actual trading skills. The course is designed to:
- Be "good enough" to prevent most refunds
- Be vague enough to enable blame-shifting
- Create desire for upgrades
- Extract maximum revenue while delivering minimal value

This is why the business model is so profitable: production cost is ~$5,000 one-time, but each customer pays $97-$25,000 for repackaged free information and false hope.
