## Why Students Don't Succeed

### Success Rate Reality

**Day Trading Industry Statistics:**
- 90% of day traders lose money (academic studies)
- 95% quit within first year
- 1% make consistent profits
- Median day trader loses ~$9,000

**TLAMA Success Rate (Estimated):**
- 95%+ of students never make consistent profit
- 80%+ never make a single profitable trade
- 60%+ never even start trading (just watch videos)
- <1% succeed (would likely have succeeded anyway)

### Reasons for Failure

**1. Trading Itself is Extremely Difficult**
- High failure rate regardless of education
- Markets are efficient (hard to beat)
- Requires significant capital, time, and skill
- Psychology is hardest part (no course can fix)

**2. Course Content is Inadequate**
- Too generic to be actionable
- No specific rules to follow
- Basic TA patterns don't provide edge
- No real risk management system
- No position sizing methodology

**3. False Expectations Created**
- Marketing promises $5K/week profits
- Reality is 90% lose money
- Students expect quick results
- Disappointment inevitable

**4. No Real Support**
- Community is inactive
- No personal feedback on trades
- No one reviewing their strategy
- Feels alone and lost

**5. Insufficient Capital**
- Many start with $500-1,000 (marketing says ok)
- Reality: Hard to make meaningful income without $25K+
- PDT rule limits day trading under $25K
- Small accounts face high fees relative to size

**6. Time Requirements Understated**
- Marketing says "90 minutes per day"
- Reality: Years to become profitable
- Thousands of hours of screen time
- Constant learning and adaptation

**7. Market Reality**
- Most patterns don't work consistently
- Markets change constantly
- Edge degrades over time
- Institutional traders have better tools/info

### The Blame Game

**When Students Fail, TLAMA Blames:**
- "You didn't follow the system"
- "You let emotions control you"
- "You didn't put in the work"
- "You need to upgrade to Pro/Elite"
- "You gave up too soon"

**Reality:**
- System is too vague to follow
- Emotions are inherent to trading (can't eliminate)
- No amount of work makes bad strategies good
- Higher tiers don't provide real edge
- 90% failure rate is normal, not individual's fault

## Refund Rate Management Tactics

### Why Refund Rates Stay Low (5-10%) Despite Poor Value

**1. Just Good Enough Content**
- Professional production quality
- Some actual information (though free elsewhere)
- Prevents "obvious scam" refund claims

**2. 7-Day Policy with Friction**
- Must email (no instant refund button)
- "Are you sure?" sequence
- Slow processing (30 days)
- Make it annoying

**3. Psychological Barriers**
- Embarrassment admitting mistake
- Cognitive dissonance (justify purchase)
- Sunk cost (already invested time watching)
- Hope (maybe it will work)

**4. Terms of Service**
- Requires "proof of effort"
- "Results not typical" disclaimer
- No guarantees (education only)
- Arbitration clause

**5. Community Pressure**
- Other students defend product (justify own purchase)
- Criticism called "negativity"
- "You just didn't try hard enough"

**6. Blame Shifting Built Into Product**
- Vague enough that failure is student's fault
- "You didn't follow the system" (system is vague)
- "You let emotions control you"
- "You need to upgrade" (more money)

## ETHICS NOTE

This value analysis reveals the core economic deception:

**What's Sold:** "Life-changing trading education, $3,700+ value"

**What's Delivered:** $60-100 of free information repackaged

**Price Charged:** $97 to $25,000+

**Value Gap:** Customer loses 90-99% of money paid when accounting for actual value delivered

### The Success Rate Deception

**What's Implied:** "You can succeed with this system, many students have"

**Reality:** 95%+ of students never profit consistently (matching industry failure rates)

**Why Not Disclosed:** Would eliminate most sales

### The Economic Harm

**Average Student Loss:**
- Starter: -$300 to -500
- Pro: -$1,200 to -$1,800
- Elite: -$10,000 to -$12,000
- Inner Circle: -$20,000 to -$25,000

**Aggregate Harm (10,000 students):**
- Even if only 30% try trading with capital
- Average loss per trading student: ~$2,000
- Total student losses: ~$6,000,000+
- Meanwhile, TLAMA profits: ~$3,500,000

**The Wealth Transfer:**
- Money flows from struggling individuals to guru
- Students lose in markets AND on course
- Guru profits regardless of student outcomes
- Business model depends on student failure (creates need for upgrades)

### Why This Matters

This isn't just "bad education" or "overpriced course." It's systematic exploitation where:

1. **False Value Claims:** $3,700 claimed for $60 content (60x inflation)
2. **Hidden Failure Rates:** 90%+ failure concealed by cherry-picked testimonials
3. **Predatory Pricing Ladder:** Extract up to $33,000+ for free information
4. **Blame Shifting:** Design enables blaming victims for inevitable failure
5. **Economic Harm:** Students lose money on course AND trading capital
6. **Opportunity Cost:** Time and money wasted on bad education

**Question:** If 95% of students fail, is this education or exploitation?

**Answer:** When failure rate matches general population (90% of traders fail), and course provides no edge, it's selling false hope rather than education. The fact that it's profitable precisely because it doesn't work (creates need for upgrades) makes it predatory by design.
