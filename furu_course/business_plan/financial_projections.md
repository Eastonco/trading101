## Key Takeaways

### Why These Numbers Work

1. **Digital products:** Zero marginal cost
2. **Psychological tactics:** Charge premium prices
3. **Minimal delivery:** Keep costs extremely low
4. **Scale quickly:** Before reputation damage
5. **No accountability:** Sustain poor customer outcomes

### The Ethical Cost

These projections demonstrate that **unethical businesses are more profitable** because:
- They don't bear costs of actual value delivery
- They exploit rather than serve customers
- They externalize harm (customer debt, broken dreams)
- They exit before accountability

**The customers funding these profits:**
- Often go into debt for courses
- Blame themselves for "failure"
- Rarely seek refunds (embarrassment)
- Don't report to authorities
- Sometimes become affiliates (trying to recoup losses)

### What This Teaches

This financial model proves that ethics and profits are often in tension. The TLAMA model works *because* it's unethical—a legitimate business with real value delivery cannot achieve these margins or returns.

**The systemic issue:** Our economic system rewards exploitation when:
- Information asymmetry exists (can't evaluate before buying)
- Regulation is weak (education space largely unregulated)
- Social proof is manipulable (fake testimonials)
- Victims blame themselves (don't seek accountability)
- Platforms profit from engagement (don't police scams)
