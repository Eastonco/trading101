# Chapter 38: Crypto Investing & Trading

## Overview

Understanding cryptocurrency concepts is one thing; actually buying, storing, and managing crypto is another. This chapter covers the practical aspects of crypto investing: choosing exchanges, securing your assets, executing trades, and managing the unique challenges of a 24/7, highly volatile market.

---

## Choosing an Exchange

### Centralized Exchanges (CEX)

**Major regulated exchanges:**

| Exchange | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Coinbase | US-regulated, insured, beginner-friendly | Higher fees, limited coins |
| Kraken | Good security track record, staking | Less user-friendly |
| Gemini | Strong compliance, SOC certifications | Limited selection |
| Binance.US | Low fees, more coins | Regulatory concerns |

### What to Look For

**Security:**
- History of hacks?
- Cold storage practices?
- Insurance coverage?
- 2FA requirements?

**Regulation:**
- Licensed in your jurisdiction?
- KYC/AML compliance?
- Transparent operations?

**Fees:**
- Trading fees (maker/taker)
- Withdrawal fees
- Deposit fees

**Features:**
- Available coins
- Trading tools
- Staking options
- Fiat on/off ramps

### Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)

**For more advanced users:**
- Uniswap (Ethereum/L2s)
- Raydium (Solana)
- PancakeSwap (BNB Chain)

**Pros:** No KYC, self-custody, more token selection
**Cons:** Higher complexity, scam tokens, gas fees, no customer support

## Account Setup

### Know Your Customer (KYC)

**Most regulated exchanges require:**
- Government ID (passport, driver's license)
- Proof of address
- Social Security Number (US)
- Selfie/video verification

**Timeline:** Verification can take hours to days

### Security Setup

**Essential steps:**
1. **Strong unique password** (use a password manager)
2. **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** - Use authenticator app, NOT SMS
3. **Whitelist withdrawal addresses** (if available)
4. **Email security** - Secure your email with 2FA too

### Funding Your Account

**Methods:**
- Bank transfer (ACH): Free/low fee, 3-5 days
- Wire transfer: Faster, higher fees
- Debit card: Instant, highest fees (3-5%)
- Crypto transfer: From another wallet/exchange

## Buying Cryptocurrency

### Order Types

**Market order:** Buy immediately at current price
- Pros: Guaranteed execution
- Cons: May get poor price in volatile market

**Limit order:** Buy only at your specified price or better
- Pros: Control your entry price
- Cons: May not execute if price doesn't reach

**Recurring buy:** Automatic purchases (DCA)
- Pros: Removes emotion, builds position over time
- Cons: Fixed schedule may not be optimal

### Fee Considerations

**Example fee impact (1% fee):**
- Buy $1,000 of Bitcoin
- Fee: $10
- You receive $990 worth of Bitcoin

**Over time:**
- 12 monthly buys × $10 = $120/year in fees
- Seek lower-fee options or higher-tier accounts

### First Purchase Checklist

- [ ] Account verified
- [ ] 2FA enabled
- [ ] Funds deposited
- [ ] Understand the fees
- [ ] Know your exit strategy
- [ ] Start small (test transaction)

## Storage and Security

### Exchange Custody

**Leaving crypto on exchange:**
- Convenient for trading
- Exchange provides security (but they hold keys)
- Risk: Exchange hack or insolvency

**"Not your keys, not your coins"** - If the exchange fails, your crypto may be lost.

### Self-Custody Options

**Hot wallets (software):**
- MetaMask (browser/mobile)
- Trust Wallet (mobile)
- Exodus (desktop/mobile)

**Cold wallets (hardware):**
- Ledger (most popular)
- Trezor (open source)
- BitBox (Swiss quality)

### Hardware Wallet Setup

**Steps:**
1. Buy directly from manufacturer (never secondhand)
2. Set up and generate new seed phrase
3. Write down seed phrase on paper (multiple copies)
4. Store seed phrases in separate secure locations
5. Never store seed phrase digitally
6. Test recovery process with small amount

### Seed Phrase Security

**DO:**
- Write on paper or metal (fire/water resistant)
- Store in multiple secure locations
- Consider a safety deposit box
- Memorize if possible (as backup)

**DON'T:**
- Store in cloud (Google Drive, iCloud)
- Take photos of seed phrase
- Store on computer/phone
- Share with anyone ever
- Enter on any website

### Security Best Practices

**General:**
- Use dedicated email for crypto
- Enable all security features
- Verify wallet addresses character by character
- Test with small amounts first
- Be paranoid (it's warranted)

**Avoiding scams:**
- No one legitimate asks for seed phrase
- "Send crypto to double it" is always a scam
- Customer support won't DM you
- Verify URLs carefully (phishing sites)

## Trading Crypto

### 24/7 Market Dynamics

**Differences from stocks:**
- Never closes
- Weekends can be volatile
- No circuit breakers
- Global news affects prices anytime

**Practical implications:**
- Set price alerts instead of watching constantly
- Use limit orders for entries/exits
- Have a plan for various scenarios
- Don't let 24/7 access create obsession

### Volatility Management

**Expect extreme moves:**
- 10-20% daily moves are normal
- 50-80% drawdowns occur regularly
- Capitulation events can be sudden

**Coping strategies:**
- Size positions for worst-case scenarios
- Don't check prices constantly
- Have predetermined buy/sell levels
- Avoid leverage (especially as beginner)

### Trading Strategies

**Dollar-Cost Averaging (Recommended for most):**
- Fixed amount at regular intervals
- Ignores price (buys high and low)
- Removes emotional decisions
- Works well for volatile assets

**Lump sum:**
- Invest full amount immediately
- Best if confident in long-term appreciation
- Harder psychologically if price drops

**Active trading:**
- Requires significant skill and time
- Most lose money (same as stocks, but worse)
- 24/7 market makes it exhausting

## Tax Implications

### Crypto Is Taxable

**In the US (and most countries), crypto transactions create tax events:**
- Selling crypto for fiat
- Trading crypto for crypto
- Spending crypto on goods/services
- Receiving crypto as income

### Tax Treatment

**Capital gains:**
- Short-term (held < 1 year): Ordinary income rates
- Long-term (held > 1 year): 0%, 15%, or 20%

**Income:**
- Mining rewards
- Staking rewards
- Airdrops
- Crypto received for work

### Record Keeping

**Track for every transaction:**
- Date acquired
- Date sold/traded
- Cost basis
- Proceeds
- Gain/loss

**Tools:**
- CoinTracker
- Koinly
- CryptoTrader.Tax
- Export from exchange when possible

### Tax-Loss Harvesting

**The wash sale rule doesn't currently apply to crypto** (as of 2024):
- Sell at a loss
- Immediately rebuy
- Claim the loss
- Maintain position

**Note:** Tax laws change. Consult a tax professional.

### Important Tax Considerations

- Every crypto-to-crypto trade is taxable
- High trading volume = complex tax situation
- Keep detailed records from day one
- Consider holding > 1 year for long-term rates

## Staking

### What Is Staking?

**Locking up crypto to support network operations and earn rewards.**

### Where to Stake

**On exchange:**
- Coinbase, Kraken offer staking
- Easy but exchange keeps some rewards
- Counterparty risk

**Native staking:**
- Stake directly through protocol
- Higher rewards typically
- More complexity

### Staking Rewards

| Crypto | Typical APY | Lock-up |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Ethereum | 3-5% | Variable (withdrawal queue) |
| Solana | 5-7% | ~2 days |
| Cardano | 3-5% | None |
| Cosmos | 15-20% | 21 days |

*APY varies with network conditions*

### Staking Risks

- Slashing (lose stake for validator errors)
- Lock-up periods (can't sell during crashes)
- Opportunity cost (tied up capital)
- Protocol risk (bugs, exploits)

## DeFi Basics (Careful Here)

### Common DeFi Activities

**Lending:**
- Deposit crypto to earn interest
- Protocols: Aave, Compound

**Providing liquidity:**
- Deposit pairs to liquidity pools
- Earn trading fees
- Risk: Impermanent loss

**Yield farming:**
- Move funds between protocols for best returns
- Complex, high risk, gas-intensive

### DeFi Risks

**Smart contract risk:** Bugs can drain your funds
**Rug pulls:** Developers steal liquidity
**Impermanent loss:** LP tokens worth less than just holding
**Complexity:** Easy to make expensive mistakes

### DeFi Recommendation for Beginners

**Don't.** Until you deeply understand the risks:
- Start with just buying and holding
- Learn about smart contract risks
- Only use battle-tested protocols
- Start with tiny amounts when you do try

## Building a Crypto Portfolio

### Allocation Framework

**Conservative:**
- 70-90% BTC
- 10-30% ETH
- 0% altcoins

**Moderate:**
- 50-60% BTC
- 25-35% ETH
- 10-15% top altcoins

**Aggressive:**
- 30-40% BTC
- 25-35% ETH
- 30-40% altcoins

### Rebalancing

**Crypto portfolios drift quickly:**
- 50% BTC can become 30% or 70% in months
- Rebalance quarterly or when drifts 10%+
- Creates tax events (plan accordingly)

### Sizing Within Total Portfolio

**Crypto as % of total investments:**
- Conservative: 1-3%
- Moderate: 3-7%
- Aggressive: 7-15%
- Speculation: 15%+ (high risk)

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Starting Too Big

**Mistake:** Putting significant savings into crypto immediately
**Better:** Start small, learn, then scale if appropriate

### 2. Chasing Pumps

**Mistake:** Buying after something has already risen 500%
**Better:** Have a strategy and stick to it

### 3. No Security

**Mistake:** No 2FA, weak passwords, seed phrase in notes app
**Better:** Treat security as critical from day one

### 4. Panic Selling

**Mistake:** Selling during crashes due to fear
**Better:** Have a plan before volatility hits

### 5. Overtrading

**Mistake:** Constantly buying/selling based on short-term moves
**Better:** Set strategy and stick to it

### 6. Ignoring Taxes

**Mistake:** Not tracking transactions for taxes
**Better:** Track everything from the start

## Key Takeaways

- **Choose reputable, regulated exchanges** for fiat on-ramps
- **Enable all security features** - 2FA is mandatory, not optional
- **Consider self-custody** for significant amounts
- **Seed phrase security is critical** - Lose it and lose your crypto forever
- **Start with DCA into BTC/ETH** - Boring but effective
- **The market never sleeps** - Use alerts and limits, not obsessive checking
- **Track every transaction for taxes** - Start from day one
- **Avoid DeFi until you deeply understand it** - Losses happen fast
