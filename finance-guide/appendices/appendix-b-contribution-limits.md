# Appendix B: Contribution Limits Quick Reference

Current limits for tax-advantaged accounts. These limits typically adjust annually for inflation.

*Last Updated: 2025 Tax Year*

---

## Retirement Account Limits

### 401(k), 403(b), Most 457 Plans

| Category | 2025 Limit |
|----------|------------|
| Employee contribution | $23,500 |
| Catch-up (age 50-59, 64+) | $7,500 |
| Catch-up (age 60-63) | $11,250 |
| Total employee (under 50) | $23,500 |
| Total employee (age 50-59, 64+) | $31,000 |
| Total employee (age 60-63) | $34,750 |
| Combined employee + employer limit | $70,000 |
| Combined limit (with catch-up, 50-59, 64+) | $77,500 |
| Combined limit (with catch-up, 60-63) | $81,250 |

**Note:** The enhanced catch-up for ages 60-63 is a new provision starting in 2025.

### Traditional and Roth IRAs

| Category | 2025 Limit |
|----------|------------|
| Contribution limit (under 50) | $7,000 |
| Catch-up contribution (50+) | $1,000 |
| Total contribution (50+) | $8,000 |

**Combined limit:** Traditional and Roth IRA contributions combined cannot exceed the limit.

### Roth IRA Income Limits (2025)

**Modified AGI Phase-Out:**

| Filing Status | Phase-Out Range | Full Phase-Out Above |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Single/Head of Household | $150,000 - $165,000 | $165,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $236,000 - $246,000 | $246,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $0 - $10,000 | $10,000 |

**Above phase-out:** Use Backdoor Roth IRA strategy (contribute to Traditional, then convert).

### Traditional IRA Deduction Limits (2025)

**If covered by workplace retirement plan:**

| Filing Status | Full Deduction Below | Phase-Out Range |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Single/Head of Household | $79,000 | $79,000 - $89,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $126,000 | $126,000 - $146,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $0 | $0 - $10,000 |

**If NOT covered but spouse is covered:**

| Filing Status | Full Deduction Below | Phase-Out Range |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Married Filing Jointly | $236,000 | $236,000 - $246,000 |

**If neither spouse covered:** Full deduction regardless of income.

## SEP IRA and SIMPLE IRA

### SEP IRA (Simplified Employee Pension)

| Category | 2025 Limit |
|----------|------------|
| Contribution limit | Lesser of 25% of compensation or $70,000 |
| Compensation limit for calculation | $350,000 |

**Self-employed calculation:** ~20% of net self-employment income after SE tax deduction.

### SIMPLE IRA

| Category | 2025 Limit |
|----------|------------|
| Employee contribution (under 50) | $16,500 |
| Catch-up (50-59, 64+) | $3,500 |
| Catch-up (60-63) | $5,250 |
| Employer match | Up to 3% of compensation |
| Employer non-elective | 2% of compensation (up to $7,000) |

## Important Notes

1. **Limits adjust annually** - Check IRS announcements each October for next year's limits.

2. **Aggregate limits** - Traditional and Roth versions of the same account type share limits (e.g., Traditional 401(k) + Roth 401(k) = one $23,500 limit).

3. **Multiple employer plans** - The employee deferral limit applies across all employers (not per employer).

4. **Self-employed considerations** - Solo 401(k) and SEP calculations use net self-employment income after deductions.

5. **State taxes vary** - Some states don't recognize federal tax treatment for certain accounts.
