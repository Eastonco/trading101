## Capital Gains Tax Rates

### Long-Term Capital Gains (Assets Held > 1 Year)

**Single Filers:**

| Taxable Income | LTCG Rate |
|----------------|-----------|
| $0 - $48,350 | 0% |
| $48,351 - $533,400 | 15% |
| Over $533,400 | 20% |

**Married Filing Jointly:**

| Taxable Income | LTCG Rate |
|----------------|-----------|
| $0 - $96,700 | 0% |
| $96,701 - $600,050 | 15% |
| Over $600,050 | 20% |

### Short-Term Capital Gains (Assets Held ≤ 1 Year)

Taxed as ordinary income at your marginal tax bracket rate.

### Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)

| Filing Status | MAGI Threshold |
|---------------|----------------|
| Single/Head of Household | $200,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $250,000 |

**Additional tax:** 3.8% on lesser of:
- Net investment income, OR
- MAGI exceeding threshold

**Maximum effective LTCG rate:** 20% + 3.8% = 23.8%

## Qualified Dividend Tax Rates

Qualified dividends are taxed at long-term capital gains rates.

**To qualify, dividends must be:**
- Paid by a U.S. corporation or qualified foreign corporation
- Stock held for more than 60 days during the 121-day period beginning 60 days before ex-dividend date

**Non-qualified dividends** (REITs, MLPs, etc.) are taxed as ordinary income.

## Social Security Taxation

### Provisional Income Calculation

**Provisional Income =** AGI + Tax-Exempt Interest + 50% of Social Security Benefits

### Taxation Thresholds (2025)

**Single Filers:**

| Provisional Income | SS Taxable |
|--------------------|------------|
| Below $25,000 | 0% |
| $25,000 - $34,000 | Up to 50% |
| Above $34,000 | Up to 85% |

**Married Filing Jointly:**

| Provisional Income | SS Taxable |
|--------------------|------------|
| Below $32,000 | 0% |
| $32,000 - $44,000 | Up to 50% |
| Above $44,000 | Up to 85% |

**Note:** These thresholds are NOT indexed for inflation and have remained unchanged since 1993.

## Key Investment Tax Rules

### Wash Sale Rule

**Rule:** Cannot deduct a loss if you buy "substantially identical" securities within 30 days before or after the sale.

**Applies to:**
- Same stock or security
- Options on the same stock
- Purchases in any account (including IRA)
- Spouse's purchases

**Does NOT apply to:**
- Gains (only losses)
- Different securities (even similar ETFs may be allowed)

**Result of violation:** Loss is disallowed; basis is added to replacement shares.

### Tax-Loss Harvesting

**Annual limits:**
- Capital losses offset capital gains dollar-for-dollar
- Up to $3,000 of excess losses offset ordinary income
- Remaining losses carry forward indefinitely

### Holding Period Rules

| Holding Period | Tax Treatment |
|----------------|---------------|
| ≤ 1 year | Short-term (ordinary income rates) |
| > 1 year | Long-term (preferential rates) |

**Options specifics:**
- Short puts/calls: Short-term regardless of time held
- Long options: Holding period starts at purchase
- Exercised options: Holding period starts at exercise

### Cost Basis Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| FIFO | First In, First Out (default) |
| LIFO | Last In, First Out |
| Specific Identification | Choose which shares to sell |
| Average Cost | Average of all purchases (mutual funds only) |

**Recommendation:** Use Specific Identification for flexibility in managing gains/losses.

## Estate and Gift Tax

### 2025 Limits

| Category | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Annual gift exclusion | $19,000 per recipient |
| Lifetime exemption | $13.99 million |
| Maximum estate tax rate | 40% |

### Step-Up in Basis

**At death, heirs receive assets at fair market value** (stepped-up basis), eliminating capital gains on appreciation during decedent's life.

**Example:**
- Original purchase: $50,000
- Value at death: $500,000
- Heir's basis: $500,000 (not $50,000)
- Heir sells immediately: $0 gain

**Implication:** Consider holding highly appreciated assets until death rather than selling.

## Estimated Tax Payments

### When Required

You may need to make estimated payments if:
- You expect to owe $1,000+ when filing
- Your withholding will be less than 90% of current year tax, OR less than 100% of prior year tax (110% if AGI > $150,000)

### Due Dates

| Quarter | Income Period | Due Date |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Q1 | Jan 1 - Mar 31 | April 15 |
| Q2 | Apr 1 - May 31 | June 15 |
| Q3 | Jun 1 - Aug 31 | September 15 |
| Q4 | Sep 1 - Dec 31 | January 15 |

### Penalty Avoidance

**Safe harbor:** Pay 100% of prior year tax (110% if AGI > $150,000) to avoid underpayment penalty.
