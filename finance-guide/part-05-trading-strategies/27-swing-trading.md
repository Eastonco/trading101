# Chapter 27: Swing Trading

## Overview

Swing trading occupies the middle ground between long-term investing and day trading. Positions are held for days to weeks, aiming to capture "swings" in price as stocks oscillate between support and resistance levels. This approach requires technical analysis skills, disciplined risk management, and the time to monitor positions—but doesn't demand watching screens all day like day trading.

---

## What Is Swing Trading?

### Definition

**Swing trading:** Holding positions for 2-20 trading days to profit from short-to-medium term price movements.

**The concept:** Stocks don't move in straight lines. Even in uptrends, there are pullbacks. Even in downtrends, there are rallies. Swing traders aim to capture these predictable moves.

### Swing Trading vs. Other Styles

| Aspect | Investing | Swing Trading | Day Trading |
|--------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Holding period | Months to years | Days to weeks | Minutes to hours |
| Trades per month | 0-2 | 5-20 | 20-100+ |
| Analysis focus | Fundamentals | Technical | Technical |
| Time required | 1-2 hrs/week | 1-2 hrs/day | 6+ hrs/day |
| Capital needed | Any amount | $25,000+ ideal | $25,000+ (PDT rule) |
| Overnight risk | Accepted | Managed | Avoided |

### Why Swing Trade?

**Advantages:**
- Captures meaningful moves without constant monitoring
- Larger profit targets than day trading
- Doesn't require quitting your job
- Overnight gaps can work in your favor
- More time to analyze and plan

**Challenges:**
- Overnight risk (gaps against you)
- Requires patience to wait for setups
- Must manage emotions over days
- Need solid technical analysis skills

## Core Swing Trading Concepts

### Following the Trend

**The mantra:** "The trend is your friend."

**In uptrends:**
- Buy pullbacks to support
- Higher probability of continuation
- Let winners run

**In downtrends:**
- Short rallies to resistance
- Or avoid (harder for most traders)
- Don't try to catch falling knives

### Support and Resistance

**Support levels:**
- Price zones where buying emerges
- Previous lows, moving averages
- Buy near support with tight stops

**Resistance levels:**
- Price zones where selling emerges
- Previous highs, round numbers
- Sell/short near resistance or wait for breakout

### Swing Points

**Swing high:** A peak surrounded by lower highs
**Swing low:** A trough surrounded by higher lows

These create the wave pattern that swing traders exploit.

## Technical Tools for Swing Trading

### Moving Averages

**Key moving averages:**
- 20 EMA: Short-term trend
- 50 SMA: Medium-term trend
- 200 SMA: Long-term trend

**How to use:**
- Buy pullbacks to rising 20 or 50 EMA
- Avoid stocks below falling 200 SMA
- Moving average crossovers signal trend changes

### Relative Strength Index (RSI)

**RSI 14 period:**
- Above 70: Overbought (potential pullback)
- Below 30: Oversold (potential bounce)
- Look for divergences between price and RSI

**Swing trading application:**
- Buy when RSI bounces from 30-40 zone
- Sell/short when RSI fails at 60-70 zone
- Use with trend direction

### MACD

**Moving Average Convergence Divergence:**
- Signal line crossovers indicate momentum shifts
- Histogram shows momentum strength
- Divergences warn of potential reversals

### Volume

**Volume confirms moves:**
- High volume on breakouts = legitimate
- Low volume on pullbacks = healthy
- High volume on pullbacks = potential reversal

## Swing Trading Setups

### 1. Pullback to Support

**Setup:**
- Stock in established uptrend
- Pulls back to support (moving average, trendline, prior resistance)
- Shows signs of stabilizing

**Entry:** When price bounces from support
**Stop:** Below the support level
**Target:** Previous high or measured move

**Example:**
- Stock trending up, at $50
- Pulls back to 20 EMA at $45
- Buy at $46 as it bounces
- Stop at $43 (below support)
- Target $52 (prior high)
- Risk: $3, Reward: $6 (2:1)

### 2. Breakout

**Setup:**
- Stock consolidating in a range
- Clear resistance level identified
- Volume building or pattern tightening

**Entry:** Break above resistance with volume
**Stop:** Below the breakout level or pattern low
**Target:** Measured move (pattern height added to breakout)

**Example:**
- Stock ranges between $40-45 for weeks
- Breaks above $45 with high volume
- Buy at $46
- Stop at $43
- Target $50 (measured move)

### 3. Reversal at Support

**Setup:**
- Stock in downtrend hits major support
- Shows reversal candlestick pattern
- Volume spike on reversal

**Entry:** After confirmation (higher close)
**Stop:** Below the support level
**Target:** Next resistance level

**Higher risk setup—require strong confirmation.**

### 4. Moving Average Bounce

**Setup:**
- Stock consistently respecting a moving average
- Pulls back to touch that MA
- Previous bounces have been reliable

**Entry:** At or slightly below the MA
**Stop:** Below the MA by buffer amount
**Target:** Previous swing high

## Entry and Exit Strategies

### Entry Techniques

**Limit orders:**
- Place buy order at support level
- May miss the trade if price doesn't reach
- Better entry price when it works

**Buy stop orders:**
- Buy above current price on breakout
- Confirms price moving in your direction
- May get filled at higher price

**Scaling in:**
- Buy half position initially
- Add if trade works
- Reduces average entry if wrong
- Reduces full position if right

### Exit Strategies

**Fixed target:**
- Set profit target at entry
- Remove emotion from decision
- May leave money on table

**Trailing stop:**
- Move stop up as price increases
- Lock in profits while staying in trend
- May get stopped out of good trade

**Scale out:**
- Sell 1/3 at first target
- Sell 1/3 at second target
- Let final 1/3 run with trailing stop

### Risk Management

**Position sizing formula:**

Position Size = (Account × Risk %) / (Entry - Stop)

**Example:**
- Account: $50,000
- Risk per trade: 1% ($500)
- Entry: $50
- Stop: $47 ($3 risk per share)
- Position size: $500 / $3 = 166 shares

**Maximum 166 shares** keeps loss to $500 if stopped out.

## Creating a Trading Plan

### Pre-Trade Checklist

Before every trade, confirm:

- [ ] Is the stock in an established trend?
- [ ] Is there a clear setup (pullback, breakout, etc.)?
- [ ] Where is support/resistance?
- [ ] What is my entry price?
- [ ] What is my stop loss?
- [ ] What is my target?
- [ ] What is my risk/reward ratio (2:1 minimum)?
- [ ] How many shares based on position sizing?
- [ ] Any earnings or events coming?

### Trade Journal

Track every trade:
- Date, ticker, setup type
- Entry and exit prices
- Stop loss and target
- Position size
- Result (P&L)
- Screenshot of chart
- What went right/wrong
- Lessons learned

**Review weekly** to identify patterns in your trading.

### Rules-Based Trading

**Example trading rules:**
1. Only trade stocks above 200 SMA
2. Minimum 2:1 reward/risk
3. Maximum 5 open positions
4. No trading first 30 minutes
5. Take partial profits at 1.5R
6. Trail stop to breakeven after 1R profit
7. No revenge trades
8. Stop trading after 3 losses

## Swing Trading Psychology

### Patience

**The challenge:** Waiting for setups to develop
**The temptation:** Forcing trades from boredom
**The solution:** Only trade A+ setups; have a watchlist

### Overnight Anxiety

**The challenge:** Holding through overnight gaps
**The temptation:** Exiting to avoid discomfort
**The solution:** Size positions so gaps don't matter; use stops

### Letting Winners Run

**The challenge:** Taking profits too early
**The temptation:** Locking in gains before they disappear
**The solution:** Use trailing stops; scale out approach

### Cutting Losers

**The challenge:** Holding losing trades hoping for recovery
**The temptation:** Moving stops, averaging down
**The solution:** Honor your stop ALWAYS; accept small losses

---

## Risk Considerations

### Overnight Gaps

**Risk:** Earnings, news, or market events cause large gaps

**Management:**
- Avoid holding through earnings
- Size positions assuming 10% gap possible
- Use mental stops for extreme scenarios

### News Events

**Risk:** Unexpected news moves stock against you

**Management:**
- Check news calendar before trading
- Avoid stocks with pending announcements
- Accept that some events are unforeseeable

### Drawdowns

**Reality:** Even good swing traders have losing streaks

**Expectation:**
- 50-60% win rate is good
- 5-8 losses in a row will happen
- Drawdowns of 10-20% occur

**Management:**
- Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
- Reduce size during losing streaks
- Take breaks when frustrated

## Getting Started

### Capital Requirements

**Minimum suggested:** $10,000
**Comfortable amount:** $25,000+

With $25,000+, you avoid Pattern Day Trader restrictions.

### Time Commitment

**Daily:**
- Evening: 30-60 min scanning and planning
- Morning: 15-30 min reviewing premarket
- Market hours: 30-60 min for entries/exits

**Total:** 1-2 hours per day

### Building Skills

**Start with:**
1. Paper trading (3-6 months minimum)
2. Master one setup before adding others
3. Keep position sizes small initially
4. Trade liquid stocks only
5. Review every trade

**Progress:**
- Track 50+ paper trades
- Achieve consistent profitability
- Then trade small real money
- Scale up only after sustained success

## Sample Swing Trade Walkthrough

### Setup Identification

**Stock:** XYZ
**Current price:** $48
**Context:** Uptrending, recently pulled back to 20 EMA

### Analysis

- 200 SMA: Trending up at $40 (bullish)
- 20 EMA: At $47, acting as support
- RSI: 42, bouncing from oversold
- Volume: Below average (healthy pullback)
- Pattern: Higher lows and higher highs intact

### Trade Plan

- **Entry:** $48.50 (above today's high)
- **Stop:** $45.90 (below 20 EMA)
- **Target 1:** $52 (prior swing high)
- **Target 2:** $55 (measured move)
- **Risk:** $2.60 per share
- **Reward (T1):** $3.50 (1.35R)
- **Reward (T2):** $6.50 (2.5R)

### Position Sizing

- Account: $50,000
- Risk: 1% ($500)
- Shares: $500 / $2.60 = 192 shares
- Position: 192 × $48.50 = $9,312

### Execution

- Day 1: Buy 192 shares at $48.50
- Day 3: Stock at $51, move stop to $49 (breakeven)
- Day 5: Sell 96 shares at $52 (first target)
- Day 8: Sell remaining 96 shares at $54 (trailing stop hit)

### Result

- Profit: (96 × $3.50) + (96 × $5.50) = $336 + $528 = $864
- Risk: $500
- R-multiple: 1.73R

## Key Takeaways

- **Swing trading captures multi-day price moves** between support and resistance
- **Trade with the trend** - Buy pullbacks in uptrends, short rallies in downtrends
- **Use technical analysis** - Moving averages, RSI, volume, patterns
- **Define risk before entering** - Know your stop loss and position size
- **2:1 reward/risk minimum** - Skip trades with poor risk/reward
- **Manage overnight risk** - Size for gaps, avoid earnings
- **Keep a trading journal** - Review and improve continuously
- **Start small and paper trade** - Build skills before risking significant capital
