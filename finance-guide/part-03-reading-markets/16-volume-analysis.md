# Chapter 16: Volume Analysis

## Overview

Volume is the number of shares or contracts traded during a given period. While price tells you what happened, volume tells you the conviction behind the move. Understanding volume analysis helps you distinguish sustainable moves from false signals.

---

## Why Volume Matters

### Volume as Confirmation

Price can move on thin air, but sustainable moves require participation. Volume answers:
- Is this move real or a false breakout?
- Is the trend healthy or exhausted?
- Who's in control—buyers or sellers?

### The Volume-Price Relationship

**Principle:** Volume should confirm price direction.

| Price Action | Volume | Interpretation |
|--------------|--------|----------------|
| Rising price | Rising volume | Healthy uptrend |
| Rising price | Falling volume | Weakening uptrend |
| Falling price | Rising volume | Strong selling pressure |
| Falling price | Falling volume | Selling exhaustion |

---

## Volume Patterns

### Climax Volume

**What it is:** Extremely high volume, often 3-5x average, typically marking a turning point.

**Selling climax:**
- Sharp drop on massive volume
- Often marks panic bottom
- Buyers absorb all selling
- Potential reversal zone

**Buying climax:**
- Sharp rally on massive volume
- Often marks euphoric top
- Sellers absorb all buying
- Potential reversal zone

### Dry-Up Volume

**What it is:** Volume declining to very low levels.

**In uptrends:**
- Low volume pullbacks are healthy
- Means sellers aren't interested
- Buyers likely to resume control

**In downtrends:**
- Low volume bounces are weak
- Means buyers aren't interested
- Sellers likely to resume control

### Volume Spikes

**Isolated high volume days:**
- Often signal important information
- Can mark reversals
- Can mark breakouts
- Pay attention to what price did that day

## Volume and Trends

### Healthy Uptrend Volume Pattern

```
Price:  ↗ ↘ ↗ ↘ ↗ ↗
Volume: ▅ ▂ ▅ ▂ ▆ ▇
        H  L  H  L  H  H
```
- Volume increases on up moves
- Volume decreases on pullbacks
- Shows buyers more aggressive than sellers

### Unhealthy Uptrend Volume Pattern

```
Price:  ↗ ↘ ↗ ↘ ↗
Volume: ▇ ▅ ▃ ▅ ▂
```
- Volume declining on rallies
- Volume steady or rising on pullbacks
- Warning sign: buyers losing interest

### Accumulation

**Definition:** Smart money (institutions) buying shares over time without pushing price up significantly.

**Signs:**
- Price flat or slowly rising
- Volume gradually increasing
- Quiet price action masks buying
- Often precedes breakout

### Distribution

**Definition:** Smart money selling shares over time without pushing price down significantly.

**Signs:**
- Price flat or slowly declining
- Volume gradually increasing
- Quiet price action masks selling
- Often precedes breakdown

## Volume at Key Levels

### Breakout Volume

**Valid breakout:**
- Price breaks resistance
- Volume significantly higher than average (1.5x+)
- Strong close near highs of day
- Follow-through next day

**False breakout:**
- Price breaks resistance
- Volume is average or below
- Price closes well off highs
- Fails back below resistance

### Support/Resistance Volume

**High volume at support:**
- Strong buying interest
- Support likely to hold
- Good entry zone

**High volume at resistance:**
- Strong selling interest
- Resistance likely to hold
- Good exit zone

**Low volume test of level:**
- Lack of interest at that price
- May break through easily
- Watch for volume surge to confirm direction

## Volume Indicators

### Volume Moving Average

Compare current volume to average volume (20-day common).

**Reading:**
- Volume above average = Significant activity
- Volume below average = Lack of interest
- Use to filter meaningful days from noise

### On-Balance Volume (OBV)

**Calculation:**
- Up day: Add volume to running total
- Down day: Subtract volume from running total

**Interpretation:**
- OBV rising with price = Bullish confirmation
- OBV falling with price = Bearish confirmation
- OBV diverging from price = Warning sign

**OBV Divergence:**
- Price makes higher high, OBV makes lower high = Bearish
- Price makes lower low, OBV makes higher low = Bullish

### Accumulation/Distribution Line

**Similar to OBV but weighted by where price closes within its range.**

Close near high = Most volume counted as buying
Close near low = Most volume counted as selling

### Volume Price Trend (VPT)

**Multiplies volume by percentage price change.**

More sensitive to price movement than OBV.

### Money Flow Index (MFI)

**RSI but with volume weighting.**

- Overbought above 80
- Oversold below 20
- Divergences similar to RSI

## Volume Profile

### What It Is

Volume Profile shows volume traded at each price level rather than over time.

### Point of Control (POC)

Price level with the highest volume—often acts as magnet for price.

### Value Area

Price range containing 70% of volume—defines "fair value."

### High Volume Nodes

Prices where lots of trading occurred—tend to act as support/resistance.

### Low Volume Nodes

Prices where little trading occurred—price tends to move quickly through these areas.

## Practical Volume Analysis

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Compare to average:** Is today's volume significant?
2. **Confirm trend:** Does volume support the price direction?
3. **Check key levels:** What's volume doing at support/resistance?
4. **Look for divergence:** Is OBV confirming price?
5. **Note extremes:** Any climax volumes suggesting reversal?

### Volume Analysis Checklist

**For breakout trades:**
- [ ] Volume 1.5x+ average?
- [ ] Strong close near highs/lows?
- [ ] Follow-through expected?

**For trend trades:**
- [ ] Volume increasing with trend?
- [ ] Pullbacks on lighter volume?
- [ ] OBV confirming direction?

**For reversal trades:**
- [ ] Climax volume present?
- [ ] Volume divergence visible?
- [ ] Key level being tested?

## Common Volume Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring Volume Entirely

Price without volume is incomplete information.

### 2. Requiring Perfect Volume

Volume doesn't need to be perfect every day—look at the pattern over time.

### 3. Comparing Across Stocks

A stock that trades 10M shares daily has different "high volume" than one trading 100K.

### 4. Extended Hours Volume

Pre-market and after-hours volume is typically thin and less meaningful.

### 5. Index Volume

ETFs like SPY have massive volume that doesn't mean the same as individual stock volume.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Volume confirms price** - Moves on high volume are more meaningful
- **Climax volume signals reversals** - Extreme volume often marks turning points
- **Healthy trends have confirming volume** - Up on rising volume, pullbacks on light volume
- **Accumulation/distribution reveals smart money** - Watch for volume patterns before moves
- **Breakouts need volume** - Low-volume breakouts often fail
- **OBV divergence warns of reversals** - When volume doesn't confirm price, be cautious
- **Context matters** - Compare volume to the stock's own average, not other stocks
