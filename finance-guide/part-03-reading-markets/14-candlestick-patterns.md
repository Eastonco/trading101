# Chapter 14: Candlestick Patterns

## Overview

Candlestick patterns are formations of one or more candles that signal potential price reversals or continuations. Developed by Japanese rice traders centuries ago, these patterns remain valuable tools for modern traders.

This chapter covers the most important candlestick patterns—what they look like, what they mean, and how to trade them.

---

## Understanding Candlestick Psychology

### What Candles Represent

Each candle shows a battle between buyers (bulls) and sellers (bears):

- **Long green/white body:** Bulls dominated the period
- **Long red/black body:** Bears dominated the period
- **Small body:** Indecision, neither side controlled
- **Long wicks:** Rejection of extreme prices

### Context Matters

Candlestick patterns gain meaning from context:
- Where does the pattern occur? (Support, resistance, trend)
- What's the overall trend?
- Is volume confirming the pattern?

A hammer at the bottom of a downtrend means more than a hammer mid-trend.

## Single Candle Patterns

### Doji

**Appearance:** Very small body with wicks on both sides. Open ≈ Close.

```
    │
    │
   ─┼─
    │
    │
```

**Meaning:** Indecision. Neither bulls nor bears won. Potential reversal when appearing after strong moves.

**Types of Doji:**
- **Standard Doji:** Equal wicks, body in middle
- **Gravestone Doji:** Long upper wick, no lower wick (bearish at tops)
- **Dragonfly Doji:** Long lower wick, no upper wick (bullish at bottoms)
- **Long-legged Doji:** Very long wicks both ways (extreme indecision)

### Hammer

**Appearance:** Small body at top, long lower wick (at least 2x body), little/no upper wick.

```
   ┌─┐
   └─┘
    │
    │
    │
```

**Meaning:** At the bottom of a downtrend, signals potential reversal. Sellers pushed price down, but buyers fought back to close near the high.

**Requirements:**
- Must be in a downtrend
- Lower wick at least 2x the body
- Can be green or red (green is stronger)

### Shooting Star

**Appearance:** Inverted hammer—small body at bottom, long upper wick.

```
    │
    │
    │
   ┌─┐
   └─┘
```

**Meaning:** At the top of an uptrend, signals potential reversal. Buyers pushed price up, but sellers fought back to close near the low.

**Requirements:**
- Must be in an uptrend
- Upper wick at least 2x the body
- Can be green or red (red is stronger)

### Hanging Man

**Appearance:** Looks exactly like a hammer, but appears at the top of an uptrend.

**Meaning:** Despite bullish appearance, warns of potential top. Signals selling pressure emerging.

### Inverted Hammer

**Appearance:** Looks like shooting star, but appears at the bottom of a downtrend.

**Meaning:** Potential bullish reversal. Buyers attempted to push higher; confirmation needed.

### Marubozu

**Appearance:** Long body with no wicks (or very tiny wicks).

```
Bullish         Bearish
┌─────┐         ┌█████┐
│     │         │█████│
│     │         │█████│
│     │         │█████│
└─────┘         └█████┘
```

**Meaning:** Extreme conviction. Price moved strongly in one direction with no rejection. Strong continuation signal.

## Two-Candle Patterns

### Engulfing Patterns

**Bullish Engulfing:**
```
     ┌────┐
  ┌──┼────┼
  │██│    │
  └──┘    │
     └────┘
Day 1  Day 2
```

- Day 1: Red candle in downtrend
- Day 2: Green candle that completely engulfs Day 1's body
- **Signal:** Bullish reversal

**Bearish Engulfing:**
```
  ┌────┐
  │    │
  │    ├──┐
  │    │██│
  └────┼──┘

Day 1  Day 2
```

- Day 1: Green candle in uptrend
- Day 2: Red candle that completely engulfs Day 1's body
- **Signal:** Bearish reversal

**Key points:**
- The engulfing candle should be notably larger
- Works best at support/resistance levels
- Volume should confirm (higher on engulfing day)

### Harami (Inside Bar)

**Appearance:** Second candle's body is contained within first candle's body. Opposite of engulfing.

**Bullish Harami:**
- Large red candle followed by small green candle inside it
- In downtrend, signals potential reversal

**Bearish Harami:**
- Large green candle followed by small red candle inside it
- In uptrend, signals potential reversal

**Meaning:** Momentum is slowing. Potential trend change.

### Piercing Line (Bullish)

```
  Day 1    Day 2
  ┌────┐
  │████│  ┌────┐
  │████│  │    │
  │████│──│    │
  └────┘  │    │
          └────┘
```

- Day 1: Long red candle in downtrend
- Day 2: Opens below Day 1's low, closes above midpoint of Day 1
- **Signal:** Bullish reversal

### Dark Cloud Cover (Bearish)

```
  Day 1    Day 2
          ┌────┐
  ┌────┐  │████│
  │    │  │████│
  │    │──│████│
  │    │  └────┘
  └────┘
```

- Day 1: Long green candle in uptrend
- Day 2: Opens above Day 1's high, closes below midpoint of Day 1
- **Signal:** Bearish reversal

### Tweezer Tops and Bottoms

**Tweezer Bottom:**
- Two candles with matching lows
- First is bearish, second is bullish
- **Signal:** Support found, potential reversal

**Tweezer Top:**
- Two candles with matching highs
- First is bullish, second is bearish
- **Signal:** Resistance found, potential reversal

## Three-Candle Patterns

### Morning Star (Bullish)

```
  Day 1    Day 2    Day 3
  ┌────┐            ┌────┐
  │████│            │    │
  │████│   ┌─┐      │    │
  │████│   └─┘      │    │
  └────┘            └────┘
```

1. Long red candle (downtrend)
2. Small body candle (gaps down, shows indecision)
3. Long green candle (closes above midpoint of Day 1)

**Signal:** Strong bullish reversal. The "star" represents the turning point.

### Evening Star (Bearish)

```
  Day 1    Day 2    Day 3
            ┌─┐
  ┌────┐   └─┘     ┌────┐
  │    │            │████│
  │    │            │████│
  │    │            │████│
  └────┘            └────┘
```

1. Long green candle (uptrend)
2. Small body candle (gaps up, shows indecision)
3. Long red candle (closes below midpoint of Day 1)

**Signal:** Strong bearish reversal.

### Three White Soldiers (Bullish)

```
                    ┌────┐
             ┌────┐ │    │
      ┌────┐ │    │ │    │
      │    │ │    │ │    │
      │    │ │    │ └────┘
      │    │ └────┘
      └────┘
```

Three consecutive long green candles, each opening within the previous body and closing higher.

**Signal:** Strong bullish momentum. Often marks the beginning of an uptrend.

### Three Black Crows (Bearish)

The opposite of Three White Soldiers. Three consecutive long red candles, each opening within the previous body and closing lower.

**Signal:** Strong bearish momentum. Often marks the beginning of a downtrend.

### Three Inside Up/Down

**Three Inside Up:**
1. Long red candle
2. Small green candle inside (bullish harami)
3. Green candle closing above Day 1's open

**Signal:** Confirmed bullish reversal.

**Three Inside Down:** Opposite pattern for bearish reversal.

## Continuation Patterns

### Rising Three Methods (Bullish)

```
  ┌────┐               ┌────┐
  │    │ ┌─┐┌─┐┌─┐     │    │
  │    │ │█││█││█│     │    │
  │    │ └─┘└─┘└─┘     │    │
  │    │               │    │
  └────┘               └────┘
```

1. Long green candle
2. Three small red candles staying within first candle's range
3. Long green candle closing above first candle

**Signal:** Uptrend continues. The small candles were just a pause.

### Falling Three Methods (Bearish)

Opposite pattern for downtrend continuation.

## Trading Candlestick Patterns

### Entry Strategies

**Conservative:**
- Wait for confirmation candle
- Enter after pattern completes and next candle confirms direction

**Aggressive:**
- Enter on pattern completion
- Tighter stop, more potential reward

### Stop Loss Placement

**General rule:** Place stop beyond the pattern's extreme.

- **Hammer:** Below the hammer's low
- **Engulfing:** Beyond the engulfing candle's wick
- **Morning star:** Below the star's low

### Volume Confirmation

Patterns are more reliable with volume confirmation:
- Reversal patterns: Higher volume on reversal candle
- Continuation patterns: Lower volume on consolidation, higher on breakout

## Pattern Reliability

Not all patterns are equally reliable. Based on research:

### More Reliable Patterns
- Engulfing patterns (especially at key levels)
- Morning/evening star
- Three white soldiers/black crows
- Hammer (at support with volume)

### Less Reliable Patterns
- Single doji (needs confirmation)
- Harami (needs confirmation)
- Tweezers (needs context)

### Success Factors

1. **Location:** Patterns at support/resistance work better
2. **Trend:** Counter-trend patterns at extremes
3. **Volume:** Confirmation from volume
4. **Timeframe:** Higher timeframes more reliable
5. **Confluence:** Multiple signals agreeing

---

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Trading Every Pattern

Most patterns fail. Look for confluence with other factors.

### 2. Ignoring Context

A hammer in the middle of a strong downtrend has different meaning than one at major support.

### 3. No Confirmation

Single candle patterns especially need confirmation. Wait for the next candle.

### 4. Tiny Timeframes

One-minute chart patterns are mostly noise. Use 4-hour or higher for reliability.

### 5. Ignoring the Larger Trend

Counter-trend patterns against the major trend have lower success rates.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Context is everything** - Patterns matter most at key levels and trend extremes
- **Confirmation increases reliability** - Wait for the next candle when in doubt
- **Volume validates** - Strong patterns have volume confirmation
- **Reversal patterns need trends** - A hammer only works at the bottom of a downtrend
- **Multi-candle patterns are stronger** - Morning star > single doji
- **Higher timeframes are more reliable** - Daily > hourly > 5-minute
- **No pattern is 100%** - Always use proper risk management
